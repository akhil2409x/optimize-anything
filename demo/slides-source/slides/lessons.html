Key lessons

  1. Measure the baseline and profile before optimizing.
  2. Check assumptions before quoting guarantees: greedy’s 63% bound failed where submodularity did.
  3. Exact methods prove and repair; heuristics scale. Combine them.
  4. Estimates mislead: planner costs, index sizes, simulated quantum speed-ups.
  5. AI writes fast; independent checks make it correct.
